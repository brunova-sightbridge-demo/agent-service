# IRS — Publication 544: qualifying property and partial gain

Public source: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544

IRS Publication 544 explains that both the relinquished and replacement real property must be held for investment or productive business use for Section 1031 nonrecognition. A personal residence and real property held primarily for sale do not qualify under the general rule. The publication also explains that receiving money or other non-like-kind property may make an otherwise qualifying exchange partially taxable. A qualified intermediary can be used within the deferred exchange rules to avoid the taxpayer receiving the proceeds directly.

The discussion is about tax classification and process, not the merits of a particular investment.
