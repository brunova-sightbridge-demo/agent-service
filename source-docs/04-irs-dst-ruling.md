# IRS — Revenue Ruling 2004-86: Delaware statutory trusts

Public source: https://www.irs.gov/irb/2004-33_IRB

In Revenue Ruling 2004-86, the IRS analyzes a Delaware statutory trust that holds rental real property under the particular trust terms described in the ruling. Under those facts, the trust is classified as a trust for federal tax purposes, and its owners are treated as owning proportional interests in the underlying real property. Consequently, an exchange of qualifying real property for an interest in that trust can fall under Section 1031 if the other requirements of Section 1031 are satisfied. The ruling does not establish that every DST interest automatically qualifies; its facts and trust restrictions matter.
