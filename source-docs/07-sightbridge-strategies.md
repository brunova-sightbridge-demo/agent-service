# Sightbridge — Public description of its strategies

Public source: https://sightbridge.com/

Sightbridge says it designs and distributes tax-advantaged real-estate programs through the wealth-management community, working with institutional real-estate owner-operators. Its public site describes a 1031 strategy using Delaware statutory trust programs. It also describes a potential later Section 721 contribution into a REIT operating partnership after a 1031 exchange into a DST. The site explicitly characterizes that later contribution as conditional, and says it does not offer tax, legal or accounting advice. These statements describe Sightbridge's proposed strategies, not a guarantee that a transaction will occur or qualify for a particular tax result.
