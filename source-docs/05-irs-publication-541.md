# IRS — Publication 541: contributions to partnerships

Public source: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p541

IRS Publication 541 explains the general rule for contributing property to a partnership in exchange for a partnership interest: ordinarily, neither the contributing partner nor the partnership recognizes gain or loss at contribution. The publication discusses exceptions and related rules, including transactions that may be treated as disguised sales when a contribution is followed by a distribution of different property. A Section 721 contribution concerns partnership interests; it is a different mechanism from a Section 1031 exchange of real property for like-kind real property.

Whether an operating-partnership transaction receives the intended treatment depends on its actual structure and facts.
