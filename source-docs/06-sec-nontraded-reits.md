# SEC Investor.gov — Investor Bulletin: Non-traded REITs

Public source: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-89

The SEC investor bulletin distinguishes publicly traded REITs from non-traded REITs. Non-traded REIT shares can be difficult to sell because they lack an exchange market. Redemption programs may have limits, may be discontinued, and may redeem below the investor's purchase price. The prospectus describes the investment strategy, offering terms and risks. This risk information is relevant when comparing a potential interest connected to a REIT with direct real-estate ownership or a DST interest; tax deferral alone does not remove investment risk.
