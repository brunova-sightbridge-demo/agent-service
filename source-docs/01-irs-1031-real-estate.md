# IRS — Like-kind exchanges: real estate tax tips

Public source: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/like-kind-exchanges-real-estate-tax-tips

The IRS describes a Section 1031 like-kind exchange as an exchange of real property held for business or investment for other qualifying real property. When the requirements are met, the exchange generally does not require current recognition of gain or loss. Receiving cash or other non-like-kind property can cause gain to be recognized to that extent. Since the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, Section 1031 applies to real property rather than personal or intangible property. Property held primarily for sale remains outside the rule.

This page describes general tax rules. It does not decide whether an individual transaction qualifies.
